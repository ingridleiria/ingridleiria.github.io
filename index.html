MyBagRADAR, resale market intelligence · repository

2026 to present
  • A daily scrape of ten authenticated resale platforms across South Korea and Brazil, written into a longitudinal panel: one observation per listing per day, so price trajectories and time to sale stay reconstructable. The panel feeds hedonic price models, value retention, and survival models of liquidity. Trilingual product on React, Node.js and PostgreSQL with pgvector.
